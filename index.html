How it fills

One signature.
One transaction.

  1. 01

    Approve USDG once

    One approval to Permit2, the standard Uniswap contract already on Robinhood Chain.

  2. 02

    Sign the slip

    Asset, depth, size, expiry. A signature, not a deposit.

  3. 03

    The price drops in

    A keeper watches the pool. At your level, one transaction pulls your USDG, swaps it on Uniswap and sends the tokens to you.

  4. 04

    Your floor holds

    If the swap would pay less than your price, the transaction fails and nothing moves. Revoke the signature to cancel.

The fine print, up front

  • USDG onlyOrders are in USDG, on assets with real Uniswap liquidity against USDG.
  • No promiseA fill is not guaranteed. Thin liquidity, fast moves and gas can leave an order unfilled.
  • Not auditedTested with unit, fuzz, invariant and Robinhood Chain fork tests. During the beta orders are capped at 100 USDG. What can go wrong
  • No token yetWhen STINKBID launches a token, the contract address appears on this page first. Anything called STINKBID before that is not ours.
