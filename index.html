How it fills

One signature.
One transaction.

  1. 01

    Approve USDG once

    One approval to Permit2, the standard Uniswap contract already on Robinhood Chain.

  2. 02

    Sign the slip

    Asset, depth, size, expiry. A signature, not a deposit.

  3. 03

    The price drops in

    A keeper checks the pool about every 10 seconds. At your level, one transaction pulls your USDG, swaps it on Uniswap and sends the tokens to you.

  4. 04

    Your floor holds

    If the swap would pay less than your price, the transaction fails and nothing moves. Revoke the signature to cancel.

What stays yours

You keep the money.
The contract keeps the price.

  • In your wallet until it fills

    A bid is a signature, not a deposit. Your USDG stays in your wallet, and your private key never leaves it.

  • A floor the contract checks

    At the fill, the contract measures the stock that lands in your wallet. Below the minimum you signed, the whole transaction reverts.

  • Everything above the floor is yours

    If the price falls through your level, the swap pays you more than the minimum, and all of it goes to your wallet.

  • Open to check, open to fill

    The contract is verified and the code is public. Active bids are public too, so anyone can fill them, on your terms only.
