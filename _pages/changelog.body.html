What changed

25 November 2026 · v0.2.0

  • MCP server at https://stinkbid.pro/api/mcp: agents can read markets, prepare a stink bid, sign it with their own wallet and submit it. The server never holds a key. Guide: /skill.md.
  • Bids from holder wallets filled on mainnet: 10 USDG on NVDA (tx) and 5 USDG on NVDA from a ladder (tx).
  • Public review opened on GitHub issue #4.

24 November 2026

  • $STINKBID token at 0x5b2c126f4522839110b2ce9d92ff454b26bdc562. Holding 1M raises your limit to 500 USDG per bid; 5M to 2,000. No fee, no revenue share.
  • The token address is on the home page (copy on click), Official and the docs.

23 November 2026

  • Two 1 USDG test fills on mainnet (NVDA and MSFT).
  • Unit tests for the shared config and for order validation run in CI.

22 November 2026

  • Ladders: one form signs 3 or 5 bids at your depth and its multiples.
  • "The official price confirms": a bid can wait for the Chainlink price to fall as far as the pool before it becomes active.
  • "Only while US markets are closed": a bid can pause during the regular US session.
  • The keeper checks about every 10 seconds instead of once a minute.
  • The order form shows the Chainlink price, the pool's premium over it and your size's price impact.
  • Wallet picker with every installed wallet; a check that your approval covers the order before you sign.
